Crumbly: Kids ABC & 123

Toddler & Preschool Learning

Crumbly is a pretend-play bakery for children aged 2 to 7. Animal customers come in, your child mixes, bakes, decorates and serves a treat, and gets a sticker. When you're ready, switch on letters, numbers, shapes, animals or colors, and the orders quietly become little lessons.

Download on the App Store

Free to download, with a free 7-day trial of everything. For iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later), on the App Store worldwide. English for now.

  • You

    Parents choose the learning

    Turn on ABC, 123, Shapes, Animals or Colors, several at once (they take turns), or none for pure pretend play. Levels move up on their own as your child learns, or you can pick one.

  • ABC

    Letters

    Customers ask for letter cookies: letter names, then letter sounds, first sounds ("B for banana"), and simple three-letter words. Then your child writes the letter on the cookie with icing.

  • 123

    Numbers

    Count toppings aloud onto a treat, from 1–5 up to 10, match numerals to amounts, learn "one more", and count the candles on a birthday cake.

  • ★

    Shapes and animals

    A star cookie, a heart, a triangle; a cow cookie, a duck, a lion, or "the cookie that goes Moo!". Tap any animal to hear its name and sound.

  • Colors

    "Red icing, please!" Your child learns six colors, then mixes them: yellow and blue icing make green right on the treat.

  • ☺

    Calm and simple

    Friendly voices say everything, so no reading is needed. Big buttons, no timers, no scores, and no "wrong" buzzers, just gentle hints. It works offline.
