Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

The short version

  • We don't collect personal information from children or parents through the app.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no crash reporting.
  • Everything the app remembers stays on your device, and we can't see it.
  • Purchases and the free trial are handled by Apple. We never see payment details.
  • It's the same in every country where Crumbly is available.

This policy explains how Crumbly: Kids ABC & 123 ("Crumbly", "the app") handles information. Crumbly is made by HomeApps ("we", "us"). It's written for parents and guardians, in plain language, and applies wherever you download or use the app. Crumbly is offered on the App Store worldwide.

What the app keeps on your device

To remember each child's play, the app stores a small amount of information only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • A child's first name (optional; you can leave it as "Little baker"), a chosen animal, and an age range (2–3, 4–5 or 6–7). Up to three children.
  • Learning progress, such as which letters, numbers, shapes, animals and colors a child has practiced.
  • The sticker book and the app's settings (learning layers, levels, letter case, sound, and the play-time reminder).

We never ask for photos, birth dates, email addresses, phone numbers, or locations. The app doesn't use the camera, the microphone, contacts, or location services.

What we don't collect

  • No personal information of any kind is sent to us.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or device identifiers.
  • No analytics, usage statistics, or crash reports from the app.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of data, because we don't have any.

Voices and sounds

The voices in Crumbly are recordings that come with the app. If a line is ever missing, your device's built-in text-to-speech reads it, on the device. Nothing your child says or does is recorded: the app doesn't use the microphone.

Third-party services

The app contains no third-party SDKs: no ad networks, analytics or tracking tools. The app never sends anything to us. It connects to the internet only for the App Store (buying or restoring the Full Bakery and the free trial), and that connection is to Apple.

  • Apple (App Store, in-app purchase and free trial). Downloads, the optional one-time "Full Bakery" purchase, and the free 7-day trial are processed by Apple. The app asks the App Store whether the Full Bakery or the trial is active on your Apple Account. We don't receive your name, Apple Account, or payment details. Apple gives us sales totals and, only if you chose to share them with app developers in your device settings, anonymous statistics that don't identify you or your child. Apple's handling of information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Device backups. If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, iOS may include the app's on-device information in that backup. This is controlled by your device settings and Apple, not by us.

Children's privacy around the world

Crumbly is made for young children, so we built it to collect nothing. That's how we meet children's privacy laws wherever the app is available, including:

  • United States: the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and state privacy laws such as California's (CCPA/CPRA). We do not collect, use, disclose, sell or "share" personal information, and we never show targeted advertising.
  • European Union, EEA and United Kingdom: the GDPR, the UK GDPR, and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code. We collect the minimum possible (nothing), keep things private by default, and never profile children or show them advertising.
  • Elsewhere: laws such as Canada's PIPEDA, Australia's Privacy Act, Brazil's LGPD, Japan's APPI, South Korea's PIPA and India's DPDP Act. The same approach applies: no personal information leaves your device.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

The little information the app needs stays on your device, under your control:

  • To see or change a child's details, open the grown-ups area and choose Edit next to the child.
  • To delete a child and their progress, choose Edit and then Delete child. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the use of personal information we hold about you, and to complain to your local data protection authority. The only personal information we might hold is a support email you sent us. Contact us and we'll help, free of charge.

Parental gate

The play area has no ads, no links, and no store buttons. Settings, the free trial, purchases, and anything that would leave the app (such as an external link or a request to rate the app) are behind a grown-ups-only question that young children can't answer. Buying the unlock or starting the trial asks the question again, right before it happens.

If you contact us

If you email us, we'll receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you (where the GDPR applies, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in replying to you), never for marketing, and we don't share it. We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you, then delete them. Please don't include a child's personal information in an email.

Our email provider stores messages on its servers, which may be in another country, such as the United States. Where the law requires it, such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, or trackers, and loads no outside fonts or scripts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Keeping information

We don't keep any app data, because none is sent to us. Information on your device stays until you delete a child in the grown-ups area or delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we'll update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect. If a change is significant (for example, if we ever needed to collect any information), we'll explain it clearly in the app's update notes and ask for a parent's consent where the law requires it.

This policy is written in English. If we publish a translation and the two differ, the English version applies, unless the law where you live says otherwise.
